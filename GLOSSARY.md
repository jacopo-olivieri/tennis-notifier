# Glossary

**Slot**: One bookable court for one hour at a specific date and start time at Lincoln's Inn Fields.

**Watch**: A member's standing request to hear about matching slots at one chosen hour. A member usually has many. A weekly watch is a weekday and start hour that repeats every week (e.g. Tue 18:00); a single-date watch is a date and start hour (e.g. Sat 31 Oct 10:00), and it may be for a date not yet bookable. Any court matches. A single-date watch disappears once its date has passed.
_Avoid_: recurring watch, one-off watch, time window

**Member**: One of the group of friends (roughly 5–20) who manages their own watches. Membership is invite-only. A member is identified by their Telegram account.

**Owner**: The member who runs the group: the only one who can create invite links and remove members.

**Invite link**: A single-use link the owner shares to bring someone in. The first person to open it and log in with Telegram becomes a member; unused links expire after 7 days.

**Release drop**: Slots becoming bookable for the first time as the booking window advances.

**Cancellation**: A previously booked slot becoming free again, whatever the cause. Most are checkout holds expiring rather than members cancelling, and a slot can flip back and forth while it's held.

**Alert**: A message telling a member that a slot matching one of their watches is free, so they can book it themselves. The system never books. A member is alerted on every release drop and cancellation that matches a watch, at any hour, but not twice for the same slot within a short window. When a member saves new watched hours, they get one alert listing the matching slots that are already free. One alert lists every matching slot found by the same check.

**Heads-up**: An evening message telling a member that slots matching their watch become bookable at the next release drop, so they can be ready at midnight. It is sent in addition to the alert at release.

**Channel**: How a member receives alerts. In v1 the only channel is a Telegram DM from the bot.
